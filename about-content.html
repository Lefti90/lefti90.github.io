About me

Introduction:

Hey! My name is Ilkka Säkkinen. I'm a Finnish programmer/developer who has been involved with computers and technology throughout my life. I was usually the IT guy, who friends and family called, when there were problems with their computers.

Troughout my school years I always picked the optional courses that had something to do with computers and learned this and that about them. If I remember correctly, I published my first website in seventh grade, but the first time I actually did some "proper" programming was in trade school. Back then I wasn't that interested in actual programming. So my programming was mostly just the mandatory programming courses and occasional code stabbing when needed in some projects. But lets jump a little bit closer to the present day...

I studied game programming and web application development at the University of Applied Sciences JAMK. I graduated from JAMK in 2023. After that, I completed Buutti Oy's Full Stack & Cloud training, during which I started an internship at Artio Oy.

After the internship, I stayed on as an employee at Artio. While working there, I drove daily to the office in Jämsä to code. The job occasionally included customer visits as well. These visits involved tasks such as servicing or repairing machines/systems, software installations, or network configuration.

I worked at Artio for just under 1.5 years (including the internship period), but the long commute started to wear on me - otherwise I'd probably still be there.

I got a position at Fujitsu and started there in May 2025. The fixed-term contract ended on 30.4.2026.


Currently I'm studying web application development independently and working on my own game project, while looking for work.
